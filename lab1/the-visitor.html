Series order: Jack Reacher #4. Also published as Running Blind. The next book is Echo Burning.

Description(Summary):

Two women with military connections are found murdered, and the FBI suspects Reacher because the victims fit a pattern associated with his past. Agent Lisa Harper recruits him to investigate from inside the case. Reacher follows a trail of evidence that points toward a killer who understands how to exploit the investigators' assumptions.
