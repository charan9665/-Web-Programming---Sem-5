Series order: Jack Reacher #8; the story is a prequel set in 1990. The next book by publication order is One Shot.

Description(Summary):

Set in 1990, this prequel finds Military Police officer Reacher investigating the death of a two-star general in a North Carolina motel. The case becomes tangled with a missing woman, a possible cover-up, and the political upheaval of the changing Army. Reacher uncovers a secret that will shape the rest of his life and career.
