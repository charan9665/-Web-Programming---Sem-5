Description(Summary):

In Chicago, Reacher helps FBI agent Holly Johnson after she is abducted by armed men. The kidnappers take them on a dangerous journey to a remote Montana compound, where Holly's identity makes her a valuable hostage. Reacher works to protect her and uncover the captors' political ambitions while searching for a way to escape.
