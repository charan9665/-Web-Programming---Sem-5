Description(Summary):

Secret Service agent M. E. Froelich asks Reacher to test the security around Vice President-elect Brook Armstrong. Reacher assembles a small team to look for weaknesses, but soon discovers that the threat is real and carefully planned. The investigation becomes a race to identify the attacker and protect the incoming vice president.
