Description(Summary):

Reacher goes undercover at a coastal estate to investigate Zachary Beck, a suspected drug trafficker. His cover story draws him into Beck's household and puts him close to Richard, a young man with a connection to Reacher's past. As the operation grows more dangerous, Reacher must balance his mission with a personal promise and confront a long-buried memory.
