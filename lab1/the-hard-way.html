Description(Summary):

In New York, Reacher witnesses a kidnapping and is hired by the victim's employer, a wealthy businessman with a complicated past. The missing man's wife and child have been taken, and the kidnappers demand a ransom. Reacher pieces together clues from the original abduction, follows a trail across the country, and confronts the people responsible.
