Description(Summary):

In Texas, Reacher accepts a ride from Carmen Greer, who fears her wealthy husband will kill her. She asks Reacher to help protect her and her young daughter, but the situation is more complicated than it first appears. When violence erupts at the family ranch, Reacher must determine who is telling the truth before the law and a powerful local family turn against him.
