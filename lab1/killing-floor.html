Description(Summary):

Former military policeman Jack Reacher steps off a bus in the small Georgia town of Margrave, where he is arrested for a murder he did not commit. As he investigates, he uncovers a conspiracy tied to the town's powerful residents and a counterfeiting operation. Reacher must find the truth before the danger closes in on him and the people willing to help.
