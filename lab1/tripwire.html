Series order: Jack Reacher #3. The next book is The Visitor, published in the United Kingdom as Running Blind.

Description(Summary):

Reacher is living quietly in Key West, working as a pool cleaner, when a private investigator asks him to find a missing woman connected to his former military unit. The search leads to a wealthy family, an old war secret, and a killer targeting women with military backgrounds. Reacher follows the trail across the country to stop the next attack.
