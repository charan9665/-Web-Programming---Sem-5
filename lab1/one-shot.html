Series order: Jack Reacher #9. The next book is The Hard Way.

Description(Summary):

A sniper kills five people in an Indiana city, and the evidence appears to point directly to former Army marksman James Barr. Barr refuses to speak to investigators and asks for Reacher, who once crossed paths with him in the military. Reacher arrives expecting to prove Barr's guilt, but new evidence leads him to question the case and pursue the real story behind the shooting.
